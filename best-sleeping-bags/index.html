A good sleeping bag can make or break a night outdoors. It’s not just about staying warm, comfort, pack size, and durability all matter. Temperature ratings are the obvious starting point, but shape (mummy vs. rectangular), insulation type (down vs. synthetic), and intended use (car camping vs. backpacking) will determine which bag is right for you. Down bags are lighter and more compressible but lose insulating power when wet; synthetic bags are heavier and bulkier but handle moisture better and are often more affordable. For most three-season campers, a 15°F to 20°F bag offers the best versatility. Below we’ve rounded up five of the most trusted models for 2026, ranked by overall value and performance.

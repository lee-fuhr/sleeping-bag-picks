How we choose our picks

We don’t run timed lab trials or pretend to be field testers. Instead, we keep a running log of reputation, long-term owner feedback, and published reviews from independent gear sites, then look for patterns across seasons of real use. We ask what actually breaks, which zippers snag, whether insulation bunches up, and whether a bag keeps its loft after a year of being stuffed in a duffel. We favor bags with proven track records and honest temperature expectations. A model gets a spot on this page when it consistently earns praise from both experts and everyday owners, not because it has the flashiest fabric or the lightest spec sheet.
